# Community Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

**SPManchester Private Limited** and the maintainers of **AgentFlow** are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, harassment-free, and professional environment for everyone, regardless of age, background, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience level, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
- Demonstrating empathy, patience, and kindness toward other contributors.
- Respecting differing viewpoints, technical opinions, and life experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the open-source community and project users.
- Showing professional conduct and integrity in all interactions.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- The use of sexualized language, imagery, or inappropriate attention.
- Trolling, insulting, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment of any individual.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community maintainers from SPManchester Private Limited are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.
